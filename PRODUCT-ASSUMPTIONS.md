# Phase I — Product Assumption Document

## Deliverable 1: "What Will You Develop?" Document

### 1. What product are you planning to build?

FoodtoGood is a surplus-food sharing platform that connects restaurants, hotels, hostels, event organizers, NGOs, and volunteers. It helps provide safe, edible surplus food to people who is in need instead of allowing it to go to waste.

The platform allows food donors to list available surplus food, NGOs to request it, and volunteers to help collect and deliver it. Our goal is to make food redistribution simple, reliable, and accessible.

### 2. What problem does it solve?

Large quantities of edible food are wasted every day by restaurants, hostels, supermarkets, and events, while many people struggle to access sufficient food.

The main problems are:

- Food donors often do not know where or how to distribute surplus food.
- NGOs and community organizations may not know where surplus food is available.
- Food can become unusable if collection is delayed.

FoodtoGood help overcome these challenges by connecting donors, NGOs, and volunteers through a single platform, helping reduce food waste and improve access to food.

### 3. Who are the target users?

The primary target users are:

- **Food donors:** Restaurants, hotels, college hostels, supermarkets, bakeries, and event organizers with surplus edible food.
- **NGOs and charities:** Organizations that distribute food to people in need.
- **Volunteers:** Individuals willing to collect and deliver available food.
- **Beneficiaries:** People who receive food through participating NGOs and community organizations.

### 4. What is the main use case?

A restaurant has surplus food at the end of the day. The restaurant owner logs into FoodtoGood and creates a listing containing the food type, quantity, pickup location, and collection deadline.

Nearby NGOs can view the listing and request the food. If transportation is required, a volunteer can accept the pickup and delivery task.

The food is collected within the specified time, and the pickup is verified using a one-time code. After successful delivery, the platform updates the donation status and records the amount of food redistributed.

### 5. What are the core features?

1. **User registration and login:** Separate access for donors, NGOs, and volunteers.
2. **Surplus food listing:** Donors can add food details, quantity, photos, location, and pickup deadlines.
3. **Nearby food discovery:** NGOs can view available food donations based on location and collection time.
4. **Food request management:** NGOs can request donations and track request status.
5. **Volunteer coordination:** Volunteers can accept pickup and delivery tasks.
6. **Smart matching:** The platform can prioritize suitable NGOs based on proximity, urgency, and pickup feasibility.
7. **Pickup verification:** A one-time code can confirm that food has been collected.
8. **Donation tracking:** Users can see whether a donation is available, requested, collected, or delivered.
9. **Impact dashboard:** Displays the quantity of food redistributed and the number of successful donations.

### 6. What assumptions are you making about the users?

- Restaurants, hostels, supermarkets, and event organizers are willing to donate safe, edible surplus food.
- NGOs are interested in receiving food donations and can arrange distribution to beneficiaries.
- Volunteers are willing to assist with collection and delivery.
- Users have access to a smartphone or computer and an internet connection.
- Donors can provide accurate information about food quantity, preparation time, and storage.
- NGOs can collect or distribute food within the specified deadline.
- Users prefer a simple platform with clear instructions and real-time donation status.

### 7. What will be included in your MVP?

The Minimum Viable Product (MVP) will include only the essential features needed to test whether FoodtoGood is useful to targeted users.

- Basic registration and login.
- Separate donor, NGO, and volunteer roles.
- A form to create and view surplus-food listings.
- Essential food details, including quantity, pickup location, storage information, and collection deadline.
- A list of available donations for NGOs.
- A request and acceptance system for food donations.
- Basic volunteer pickup coordination.
- Donation status tracking.
- Simple pickup verification using a one-time code.
- A basic dashboard showing completed donations and food quantities redistributed.

### 8. What will NOT be included in the MVP?

- AI-based prediction of surplus-food quantities.
- Automatic integration with restaurant billing and inventory systems.
- Real-time GPS tracking of volunteers.
- Online payments, subscriptions, and paid delivery services.
- Expansion across multiple cities or countries.
- Dedicated mobile applications for Android and iOS; the first version will focus on a web-based platform.
- Automatic food-quality detection using cameras or sensors.

These features may be considered in future versions after validating the MVP and understanding user needs.
